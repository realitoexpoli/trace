Refund policy

Last updated: DATE

If Tracé Pro is not right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of a payment and you get the full amount of that payment back. This applies to the first payment and to each renewal.

How to ask

Reply to your Paddle receipt email, or contact Paddle through paddle.net. Paddle, our reseller and the Merchant of Record for our orders, handles all refunds.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from “Manage billing” in the app. You will not be charged again, and Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Your decks are never deleted because of a cancellation.
