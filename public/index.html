What you can show

Everything you draw on the board, animated

Every object knows maths. Change a number and the graph, the label and the value that depends on it all follow.

Equations that draw their graphs

Write y = sin(x) e^(-x/4) like on a calculator. Reveal a formula term by term while its curve closes in.

Morph one idea into the next

Shapes, equations and graphs turn into each other. Objects move along paths and arrows draw from tail to tip.

3D you can turn while presenting

Surfaces from an equation, spheres, prisms and polyhedra. Drag to rotate in front of the room.

Signals and series

Fourier series, waves, phasors, spectra and charts, ready to animate. Counters tick up as the picture builds.

Geometry that responds

Triangles, angles, number planes, vectors and Venn diagrams. Move a corner and every label follows.

Live sliders on stage

Let the audience ask “what if k doubles?” and answer by dragging a slider during the talk.

How it works

As simple as a slide deck

No timeline and no keyframes. You decide what appears on each click, and Tracé animates the rest.

  1. Add objectsText, equations, graphs, shapes, 3D, science diagrams.
  2. Choose what happens on each clickAppear, move, morph, draw, count. Or duplicate the slide, change it, and Tracé animates between the two.
  3. Present, or share a linkFull-screen with live sliders. Viewers can watch your deck on any device.
  4. Need a video?Export a ready-to-render script for Manim, the open-source engine behind many maths videos.

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Tracé runs in your web browser on a computer, tablet or phone (Chrome or Edge work best). Exporting a video uses Manim on your own computer, which is optional.

Do I need an account?

Not to make slides: your decks are kept in your browser. An account saves them online so they open on any device and can be shared with a link.

Can I present without internet?

Once the editor is open, presenting works without a connection. Decks in your account sync again when you are back online.

Can I keep my work if I stop paying?

Yes. Nothing is deleted when you cancel. You keep every deck; above 3 decks you just cannot add new ones online until you remove some or come back to Pro. You can also download any deck as a file at any time.

Is there a discount for students?

The Free plan has the whole editor, which is what most students need. For classes or departments, email us.

How do refunds work?

If Pro is not for you, ask within 14 days of paying and you get your money back. See the refund policy.
