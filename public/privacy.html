Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what Tracé collects and why. The controller of your data is Taha Nadari, Morocco, who you can reach at support@traceanim.com.

Without an account

Your decks stay in your own browser. We do not receive them.

With an account

  • Email address: to sign you in and to contact you about your account.
  • Password, if you set one: stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider. Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • If you sign in with Google or GitHub: your name, email address and account id from that service, used only to sign you in and to start your profile.
  • Your profile: your name, role, and optionally your subject and school, shown on decks you share.
  • Your decks: stored so they open on your devices, and shown to people you share a link with.
  • Number of views of shared decks, without any information about the viewers.
  • Plan and subscription status, received from Paddle.

Payments

Payments are processed by Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record for our orders. Paddle receives your payment details and billing address under its own privacy policy. We never see your card number.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database and sign-in (stored in the EU, in Stockholm, Sweden).
  • Cloudflare: hosting of the website.
  • Paddle: payments and invoices.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the open-source code libraries the editor uses.
  • Google and GitHub: only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Resend: sends sign-in and account emails.
  • Anthropic: only when you use the AI builder (see below).

The AI builder

When you use the AI builder, the text you type, the slide size and the titles of the slides already in your deck (if you add slides) are sent to Anthropic, which runs the AI model that writes the slides. We do not send your email address or other account details with it, and we do not keep a copy of your request: only the slides that come back are saved, in your deck. We keep a count of the credits you have and have used.

No ads, no tracking

Tracé shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics trackers. The site stores sign-in information and your local decks in your browser; these are necessary for the service to work.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and decks until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Paddle keeps payment records as required by tax law.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and you can object to its use, by writing to support@traceanim.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
