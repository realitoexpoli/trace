Presenting

Press Present to play the deck full screen from the slide you are on. Every click plays the next step; live sliders let you change the maths on stage.

Moving through the deck

ToDo this
Go to the next stepClick anywhere, or press →, Space, Page Down or Enter
Go back one step← or Page Up
Hide or show the live slidersC, or the button at the top left
LeaveEsc, or Exit (Esc) at the top right

A click during an animation lines up the next step, so you never lose a click. Most presentation remotes send Page Down and Page Up, so they work too.

Live sliders and 3D

A slider appears when you ticked Live slider for a letter in an equation, Slider for the point while presenting on a graph, or Turn and tilt sliders while presenting on 3D axes. Moving a slider updates everything linked to it: the equation’s graph, a counter, a 3D surface. Going to the next step resets them to the values you set in the editor.

3D objects can be turned by dragging them while presenting.

Tablets and phones

  • Swipe left for the next step and right to go back, or tap the ‹ and › buttons.
  • Tap the left quarter of the screen to go back; tap anywhere else to go forward.
  • Held sideways, sliders stay hidden so they don’t cover the slide; tap Show controls to see them.

On the day

  • Open the editor and the deck before the talk while you have internet: the maths fonts load once, then presenting works offline.
  • Play the deck once on the projector. Colours look different on projectors; light text on a dark background is safest.
  • If something goes wrong, sharing a link (next page) lets you present from any other computer.
