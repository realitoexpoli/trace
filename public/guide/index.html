The Tracé guide

Everything you need to make maths and physics slides that move. Most people are presenting their first animated slide within ten minutes.

The short version

  1. Open the editor. No account or installation needed. Your work is saved in your browser as you go.
  2. Add things from the menus at the top: Write, Draw, Geometry, Plot, 3D and Science.
  3. Type an equation such as y = sin(k x) and press Plot this equation as a graph.
  4. Decide what happens on each click. Select an object and set Appears to On click 1. Or duplicate the slide, change it, and Tracé animates the difference.
  5. Press Present and click through. Create a free account to save your projects online and share a link.
