Keyboard shortcuts

In the editor

KeysDoes
Ctrl+ZUndo
Ctrl+Shift+ZRedo
Ctrl+DDuplicate the selected object
Delete or BackspaceDelete the selected object
Arrow keysNudge the selected object (with Shift for bigger steps)
Ctrl+B, I, UBold, italic, underline for a selected text, list or table
EscDeselect
Tab / Shift+TabSelect the next / previous object
Alt + clickSelect the object under the selected one
Shift + dragTurn a 3D object

On a Mac, use ⌘ instead of Ctrl.

Typing maths

To getTypeNotes
Powersx^2, e^(-x/4)Put longer powers in brackets
Multiplication2x, k x, 2*xA space or nothing between letters multiplies
Fractions1/(1+x^2)Brackets show what is on top and below
Roots and absolute valuesqrt(x), |x|
Trigonometrysin cos tan sec csc cot, asin acos atan, sinh cosh tanhAngles are in radians
Logarithms and exponentialln(x), log(x), log2(x), exp(x)log is base 10, ln is natural
Rounding, factorialfloor(x), ceil(x), n!
Greek letters and πpi, theta, lambda, sigma…Written as the Greek letter on the slide
SubscriptsP_j, d_tEach one is its own symbol
A curve(cos(t), sin(2t))A pair in t
A vector fieldF = (-y, x)A pair in x and y
A vectorv = (2, 1)Two numbers
A circle or other curve in x and yx^2 + y^2 = 4
A 3D surfacez = sin(x) cos(y)

Letters other than the variables (x, and t or θ for curves) become parameters: each gets a value you can set, animate on a click, or turn into a Live slider. For anything this cannot express, switch Write it as to LaTeX.

When something looks wrong

I cannot select an object.
It is probably under another one. Alt+click to go down a layer, or click an empty part of the slide and pick it from the On this slide list.
I cannot find a setting.
It may be folded away: open Position, size and turn or Leaving, moving and looping in the settings.
The graph is empty or cut off.
Select the graph and press Fit ranges to the curve. Check the red note under the equation, if any.
An equation shows as code instead of maths.
The maths fonts load from the internet the first time. Reload the page once you are online.
An object appears before its animation.
Check its Appears setting: With the slide means it is there from the start.
A change animates on the wrong click.
Select that object, click the step in the clicks bar where it changes, and use Undo this click’s changes. Then redo the change on the right click.
My decks are not on my other computer.
Sign in with the same email on both. Decks marked “Device only” are not online: see the saving states.
The sign-in email does not arrive.
Check your spam folder, wait a minute and press Send it again. The link works in the browser where you asked for it.
The cloud says “Finish profile”.
Click it and fill in your name and what you do. Projects are saved online only once the profile is complete.
